## **About DevPath**
DevPath is an innovative platform designed to help individuals in the IT sector achieve their career goals through clear and structured learning paths. Our platform bridges the gap between users' current skills and market demands by providing personalized recommendations based on real-time job market data.

---

## **Goals**
- Empower users to achieve their career objectives with tailored learning paths that align with their technical aspirations.
- Bridge the gap between users' skills and job market demands by analyzing real-time data on in-demand skills and job openings.
- Reduce confusion in selecting career paths within the same field by offering clear, data-driven guidance.

---

## **Features**
1. **Official Roadmaps:**  
   Curated by DevPath editors to ensure accuracy and quality.
   
2. **AI-Generated Roadmaps:**  
   Allows users to create custom roadmaps if no predefined roadmap matches their needs.

3. **Progress Tracking:**  
   Users can monitor their progress across various stages of their selected roadmap.

4. **Course Recommendations:**  
   Suggests courses for the entire roadmap or specific topics to enhance or acquire new skills.

5. **AI Assistant:**  
   Tracks users' progress, identifies completed topics, and provides real-time guidance during their learning journey.

6. **Job Statistics:**  
   Provides users with the number of available jobs for specific topics, helping them make informed decisions when choosing between similar fields.

7. **CV Builder:**  
   Build professional CV based on users' completed roadmaps and acquired skills, saving time.

---

## **Technologies Used**
- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript.
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL.
- **Other Tools:** Vercel, GitHub, Jira, AI Models (Gemini 1.5 Flash).
